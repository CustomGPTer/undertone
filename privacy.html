Privacy policy

Undertone has no servers and no accounts of its own. What you search for and play stays between your phone and the sites the music comes from.

In short

The developer collects nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, in the app or on this website.

What stays on your phone

Your library, playlists, play history and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone.

Who the app talks to

  • Public video sites, to search for songs and play their sound. They see your phone's internet address, as with any visit.
  • lrclib.net, to fetch lyrics for the song playing.
  • sponsor.ajay.app, only if you switch on skipping non-music parts, to fetch the parts to skip.
  • Google Drive, only if you sign in for the backup.
  • GitHub, once a day, to see whether a newer version is out. Only the version number is read.

Google Drive backup

If you choose to sign in, Undertone asks only for permission to use its own hidden app data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). It saves one small file there, undertone-backup.json, with your library, playlists, settings and history, and reads it back when you restore. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive. The file is not sent to the developer or anyone else. You can delete it by removing Undertone's access in your Google account's settings.

Children

Undertone is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 21 November 2026.
