Free in Undertone, paid in the big streaming apps

The big music apps are free to start, with adverts, then charge to take the adverts away and let you choose what plays. Undertone has no paid version.

What Undertone does not do

Undertone needs an internet connection: it does not save songs for offline listening. It does not offer lossless audio, and it is an Android app only. It is not on Google Play. Its music comes from public video sites, so a song can disappear if the site removes it; Undertone flags that and suggests a replacement.
